# 花蕊夫人衙香 (Huarui Furen's Court Incense): Recipe

**Direct answer:** 花蕊夫人衙香 is a rich court 合香 attributed to **花蕊夫人 (Lady Huarui)**, the famous consort of the Later Shu ruler 孟昶. It is built on the classic court trio — **agarwood, sandalwood, and musk** — with frankincense, borneol, and ambergris-derived 甲香 for depth.

**Key facts:** 衙香 (yamen incense) was a *category* of court/office incense, not a single scent — and the 花蕊夫人 version is the most famous named one. It is a lesson in the **rich, layered court blend** as distinct from the leaner scholar blends.

---

## The Recipe (香乘 version)

> **沉香、栈香、檀香、乳香、龙脑、甲香、麝香** — agarwood (沉香), 栈香 (a non-sinking agarwood grade), sandalwood (檀香), frankincense (乳香), borneol (龙脑), 甲香 (operculum), and musk (麝香). Except the borneol, grind together; add 炭皮末 (charcoal-bark powder) and 朴硝 (saltpetre) one 钱 each; blend with raw honey; warm in a sealed vessel over boiling water (重汤) ten or so times; age (窨) seven days; form into cakes and burn.

| Role | Ingredient | Job in the blend |
|---|---|---|
| 君 (chief) | 沉香 + 栈香 agarwood (two grades) | The deep resinous backbone |
| 臣 (minister) | 檀香 + 乳香 sandalwood, frankincense | Warm wood and bright resin support |
| 佐 (assistant) | 龙脑 borneol | A cool, crystalline lift that brightens the heavy base |
| 使 (guide) | 甲香 + 麝香 + 炭皮末/朴硝 + honey | Fixatives, combustion aids, and binder |

---

## What "衙香" Means

衙香 is not one recipe — it is a **class of compound incense** used in the yamen (官署, official offices) and court. The 陈氏香谱 alone lists fifteen 衙香 recipes. The common thread: the **沉檀麝 trio** (agarwood, sandalwood, musk) at the core, meaning these were expensive, courtly blends — the opposite of the humble 柏子香.

---

## The Honest Caveats

- The 花蕊夫人 attribution is traditional, not a signed document.
- The historical 两/钱 weights varied — read the ratios, not the grams.
- 甲香 is operculum (a shellfish-derived material), a traditional fixative rarely used today; modern reproductions typically substitute or omit it.

## The Author's Take

**Position:** 花蕊夫人衙香 is the "rich blend" that shows 合香 was not always austere — the court could layer agarwood, sandalwood, musk, and borneol into something deliberately opulent.

**Reasoning:**
- The 沉檀麝 core marks it as a status blend — the materials themselves signal wealth.
- Contrast it with 柏子香 (a humble seed) and the full social range of the tradition becomes visible.

## FAQ

### Q: What is 花蕊夫人衙香?
A rich court compound incense attributed to Lady Huarui, built on agarwood, sandalwood, and musk with frankincense and borneol.

### Q: What is 衙香?
A category of court/office incense — the 陈氏香谱 lists fifteen recipes, all built on the agarwood–sandalwood–musk core.

### Q: What is 甲香?
Operculum, a shellfish-derived traditional fixative — rarely used today; modern reproductions usually substitute or omit it.

### Q: Who was 花蕊夫人?
The famous consort of Meng Chang of the Later Shu, celebrated as a poet and beauty; the incense attribution is traditional.
