# 寿阳公主梅花香 (Shouyang Princess's Plum Incense): Recipe

**Direct answer:** 寿阳公主梅花香 is a classical plum-blossom 合香 attributed to **寿阳公主 (Princess Shouyang)** of the Southern Dynasties — a legendary figure tied to the "梅花妆" (plum-blossom makeup) story. The recipe builds a **plum note from sandalwood, herbal tones, and 白梅 (white plum)**, not from the flower's scent alone.

**Key facts:** "梅花香" (plum incense) survives in *many* versions in the 香乘; 寿阳公主梅花香 is the most famous named one. The plum character is an *accord* — built from layered ingredients — not a single flower.

---

## The Recipe (香乘 version)

> **甘松半两、白芷半两、牡丹皮半两、藁本半两、茴香一两、丁皮一两、檀香一两、降真香二钱、白梅一百枚** — spikenard (甘松), angelica (白芷), peony bark (牡丹皮), lovage (藁本), fennel (茴香), clove bark (丁皮), sandalwood (檀香), rosewood (降真香), and a hundred white plums (白梅). Except the clove bark, roast everything dry, grind to a coarse powder, and age (窨) in a sealed vessel for a month before burning.

| Role | Ingredient | Job in the blend |
|---|---|---|
| 君 (chief) | 檀香 sandalwood | The warm woody body |
| 臣 (minister) | 甘松 / 白芷 / 藁本 spikenard, angelica, lovage | Herbal, slightly medicinal middle tones |
| 佐 (assistant) | 白梅 + 牡丹皮 white plum, peony bark | The tart-sweet "plum" character — the accord's signature |
| 使 (guide) | 茴香 / 丁皮 / 降真香 fennel, clove bark, rosewood | Spice and fixative notes that bind and lift |

---

## The Plum Is an Accord, Not a Flower

The recipe does not rely on plum flowers alone. The "plum" character is **constructed** — a tart-sweet note from 白梅 (pickled/dried plum) over warm wood and herbal tones. This is the same compositional logic as a perfumer building a "floral" accord from materials that are not the flower.

This is why 梅花香 has so many versions: each is a different *construction* of the same idea.

---

## The Honest Caveats

- **The 寿阳公主 attribution is legend.** The plum-blossom-makeup story (《太平御览》) is a romantic origin tale, not authorship.
- **Many versions.** The 香乘 records 梅花香一/二/三 and 寿阳公主梅花香 separately; sources do not agree on one formula.
- **Historical weights vary** — read the ratios, not the grams.

## FAQ

### Q: What is 寿阳公主梅花香?
A classical plum-blossom compound incense attributed (legendarily) to Princess Shouyang, built from sandalwood, herbal tones, and white plum.

### Q: Does it use real plum flowers?
The "plum" character is built as an accord — 白梅 (dried plum) over warm woods — rather than from the flower's scent alone.

### Q: Why are there so many "plum incense" recipes?
Because each version is a different *construction* of the plum idea; the 香乘 records several distinct 梅花香 formulas.

### Q: Did Princess Shouyang really make it?
The attribution is traditional legend (the plum-blossom-makeup story), not documented fact.
