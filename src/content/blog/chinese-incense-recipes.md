# Chinese Incense Recipes (香方): Translated from the Classical Manuals

**Direct answer:** A 香方 (xiāngfāng) is a **classical Chinese incense recipe** — a structured formula for a compound incense (合香), recorded in the canonical manuals (香谱). The recipes name the ingredients and their proportions, and the best ones reveal the 君臣佐使 (chief–minister–assistant–guide) structure behind the blend.

**Key facts:** This cluster translates a selection of classical and revival recipes into English — most of which have **almost no English coverage** anywhere. That is the point: the manuals are the deepest, least-translated part of Chinese incense culture.

---

## What a 香方 Actually Is

A 香方 is not a marketing description of a scent. It is a **recipe** — a list of materials, their proportions, and the processing method (grinding, steaming, aging, forming). The classical canon (陈氏香谱, 洪芻香谱, 香乘) is essentially a **recipe book**, hundreds of entries long.

The English internet almost entirely misses this. Searching for "Chinese incense recipe" returns modern DIY blends, not the classical canon. This cluster is a small, honest bridge.

## How to Read a Recipe Honestly

Three rules apply to every page in this cluster:

1. **Attribution is usually legend.** "苏轼," "寿阳公主," "花蕊夫人" are cultural markers, not documented authorship. Treat the name as the recipe's *title*, not its *author*.
2. **Proportions vary by dynasty.** The historical weights (两, 钱) had different gram values over time, and the same recipe survives in multiple versions. Read the *ratios*, not the exact grams.
3. **Some are modern, not ancient.** 二苏旧局 is a contemporary revival, written in the classical idiom. It is not a 香谱 original.

## How to Read the 君臣佐使 Structure

Every recipe here is deconstructed by role — **君 (chief), 臣 (minister), 佐 (assistant), 使 (guide)** — the same logic as a Chinese herbal formula. Four questions unlock any 合香 recipe:

- **What carries it?** (the chief — almost always a wood)
- **What rounds it?** (the minister — a smaller wood or resin)
- **What names it?** (the assistant — the signature note)
- **What binds it?** (the guide — often a binder or a process)

## FAQ

### Q: What is a 香方?
A classical Chinese incense recipe — a structured formula for compound incense, recorded in the canonical 香谱 manuals.

### Q: Are these recipes real or invented?
They are real, recorded in the manuals — but the famous attributions (苏轼, 寿阳公主, 花蕊夫人) are usually legend, and the exact proportions vary by version and dynasty.

### Q: Can I actually make these at home?
The recipes are reproducible in principle, but the historical weights are not fixed gram values, and some materials (musk, 奇楠-grade agarwood) are scarce or restricted. Treat them as historical study, not a modern DIY spec.

### Q: Why is there almost no English coverage of this?
The manuals are extensive classical Chinese that has never been systematically translated — which is precisely the opportunity this cluster addresses.
