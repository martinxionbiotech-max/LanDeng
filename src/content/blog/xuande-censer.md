# 宣德炉 (Xuande Censer): The Ming Collector's Censer

**Direct answer:** The 宣德炉 is the **most famous — and most copied — incense censer in Chinese history**, cast by imperial order during the **Xuande reign (宣德, 1426–1435)** of the Ming dynasty, using imported copper. Its plain, elegant form and prized patina made it the standard by which later censers were judged.

**Key facts:** The defining feature of a 宣德炉 is not ornament but **patina (皮壳)** — the surface color that develops with age. Genuine Xuande-period pieces are extremely rare; the vast majority of "宣德炉" on the market are later copies, some themselves centuries old and collectible.

---

## The Form

Unlike the theatrical 博山炉, the 宣德炉 is deliberately **plain** — a simple rounded body, often with small handles (耳), resting on short feet. The beauty is in **proportion and patina**, not carving.

| Trait | Description |
|---|---|
| Body | Simple, rounded, unadorned |
| Handles | Small loop or animal handles (e.g., 蚰龙耳) |
| Patina (皮壳) | The prized surface color from age and handling |

---

## The Famous Patinas

Collectors name the patina colors, each a sign of age and alloy. Traditional names include:

- **棠梨色** (crabapple-pear) — a warm reddish-brown
- **猪肝色** (pig's-liver) — a deep liver-brown
- **蟹壳青** (crab-shell green) — a greenish-bronze tone

These are **patina names, not a grading system** — and they are exactly what forgers imitate. A "宣德炉" with a perfect, uniform patina is usually a red flag.

---

## The Buying Reality (Honest)

Because genuine Xuande-period censers are museum-grade rarities, "buying a 宣德炉" in practice means buying a **later replica** — some Qing or even modern. The honest advice:

1. **Assume it is a copy** unless proven otherwise.
2. **Judge it as a copy** — is it a well-made bronze with a natural patina, or a mass-produced fake?
3. **Price should reflect "later copy," not "Xuande original."**

## FAQ

### Q: What is a 宣德炉?
The famous Ming-dynasty censer cast by imperial order during the Xuande reign (1426–1435), known for its plain form and prized patina.

### Q: How do I tell a real 宣德炉?
Genuine Xuande-period pieces are extremely rare — most "宣德炉" are later copies. Judge it as a copy: quality of casting and naturalness of patina.

### Q: What are the patina colors?
Traditional names include 棠梨色 (warm brown), 猪肝色 (liver-brown), and 蟹壳青 (crab-shell green) — patina names, not a grading system.

### Q: Should I buy a "宣德炉"?
Buy a well-made bronze censer at a fair price and treat "Xuande" as a style label, not a provenance claim — unless you have expert certification.
