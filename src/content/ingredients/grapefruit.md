> **Direct answer:** Grapefruit (葡萄柚, pútáoyòu) is the fruit and rind of *Citrus × paradisi*, a modern cultivated citrus hybrid in the rue family (Rutaceae). Its peel carries a fresh, bittersweet, green-slightly-woody citrus scent from its essential oil, and it is a modern essential-oil fragrance material, not a classical Chinese incense ingredient. It is distinct from the pomelo (柚), its larger ancestral parent.

## Aroma profile

> **Direct answer:** Grapefruit is fresh, bright, and bittersweet, with a green, slightly woody edge that distinguishes it from the cleaner sweetness of lemon or orange. It is a top note with more body than lemon — the bittersweet, slightly sulfurous green character comes from nootkatone and the mercaptan compounds in the rind oil.

## How is it processed?

> **Direct answer:** Grapefruit is processed by cold-pressing the rind to yield its essential oil — the bittersweet, green-woody citrus note. The oil, not the fruit or the dried peel, is the fragrance product; cold-pressing (rather than steam distillation) preserves the delicate fresh top note.

The processing is a cold-press extraction: the rind is pressed to yield the essential oil, and cold-pressing (rather than steam distillation) is used precisely because it preserves the delicate, fresh, bittersweet top note that heat would degrade. The oil is the fragrance product — not the juice, not a dried peel. For a buyer, this means the relevant quality checks are on the oil (freshness, the bittersweet-green character, and the presence of the nootkatone and sulfur compounds that give it its signature), not on a dried plant part.

## What determines quality?

> **Direct answer:** Grapefruit quality turns on the essential oil — its freshness, the bright bittersweet-green character, and the nootkatone and trace sulfur compounds that define the scent. The quality points center on the oil:

3. **Extraction** — cold-pressed oil preserves the top note; heat or poor extraction degrades it.

These are oil-quality checks, consistent with a material whose value is a cold-pressed essential oil.
