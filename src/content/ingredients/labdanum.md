> **Direct answer:** Labdanum (岩蔷薇, yánqiángwēi, "rock rose") is the resinous exudate of *Cistus ladanifer*, the gum rockrose, a shrub of the rockrose family (Cistaceae) native to the western Mediterranean. It is a deep, balsamic, leathery, amber-like resin used in modern perfumery as a base and fixative. It is a modern crossover material, not part of the classical Chinese incense canon.

## Aroma profile

Labdanum is deep and resinous, with a balsamic sweetness over a leathery, smoky, amber-like base — sometimes described as the "ambergris of the plant world" for its animalic warmth. It is a *base note and fixative*: it anchors a blend from below, adding richness and tenacity. In modern Western perfumery it is a cornerstone of amber and chypre accords.

## Botanical source and origin

*Cistus ladanifer* is an evergreen shrub of the rockrose family (Cistaceae), native to the western Mediterranean — chiefly the Iberian Peninsula and North Africa. It produces a sticky, aromatic resin (labdanum) on its leaves and twigs, traditionally gathered by combing the resin from the shrub or boiling the twigs. The family Cistaceae is unrelated to the rose family (Rosaceae); the name "rock rose" describes its habitat and rose-like flowers, not a botanical relation.

## In modern practice

- **Perfumery:** labdanum is a staple base and fixative of modern Western perfumery, central to amber and chypre accords and valued for its tenacity.
- **Amber accords:** labdanum is a key natural source of the "amber" note in perfumery, often blended with vanilla and benzoin to build the classic amber base.
- **Incense:** labdanum appears in some modern incense and resin blends through the global fragrance trade, though it is not a classical Chinese incense material.

## Traditional use vs modern evidence

Labdanum's documented use is in modern perfumery, not in classical Chinese medicine or incense. We make **no medical claims** and do not fabricate a classical Chinese pedigree. The documented facts are botanical identity, the Mediterranean origin, and the modern perfumery role.

## Quality and sourcing notes

Practical points for buyers:

- Labdanum is sold as a resin (concrete or absolute), and the quality marker is a deep, leathery, balsamic richness without harsh solvent notes.
- Recognize 岩蔷薇 as a **modern Western material**, not a classical Chinese incense ingredient — buy it for modern amber or resin accords, not as an "authentic Chinese" material.
- The term "rock rose" should not be confused with true roses (*Rosa*), an unrelated family.

## Safety

Incense combustion produces smoke and particulate matter. Burn in a well-ventilated space, avoid inhaling concentrated smoke, and keep away from children and pets. This is editorial knowledge content, not medical advice.
