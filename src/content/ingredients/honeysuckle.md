**Technical answer:** 金银花 — *jīnyínhuā* in Chinese — is the flower of *Lonicera japonica*, the Japanese honeysuckle, a twining vine of the honeysuckle family (Caprifoliaceae) native to East Asia. Its sweetly fragrant white-to-yellow flowers are best known as a materia-medica flower (the plant itself is 忍冬 *rěndōng*), and their use in incense and fragrance is a **modern crossover** rather than a classical incense-canon material. This page positions it honestly: a sweet floral with a documented materia-medica identity, not a 香乘 ingredient.

## Aroma profile

金银花 is sweet and honeyed with a green, slightly herbaceous edge — a light, airy floral closer to a honeysuckle hedge in bloom than to an opulent white flower. It is fresher and less heady than jasmine, with a clean sweetness that reads almost honey-suckle-dewy. In a blend it works as a *light sweet-floral top note*, adding a gentle honeyed lift rather than deep richness.

## The 金银花 / 忍冬 naming note

One plant, two common names:

- **忍冬 (rěndōng)** — the vine itself, *Lonicera japonica*.
- **金银花 (jīnyínhuā)** — the flower of that vine, named for its white-to-yellow colour shift ("gold-silver flower").

Both refer to the same species; 金银花 is the flower material used. The English name "honeysuckle" is shared across the genus *Lonicera*, but the Chinese incense and materia-medica reference is specifically *Lonicera japonica*.

## Botanical source and origin

*Lonicera japonica* is a twining, woody vine of the honeysuckle family (Caprifoliaceae), native to East Asia (including China, Japan, and Korea) and widely naturalised elsewhere. Its paired, tubular flowers open white and age to yellow, and are sweetly fragrant, especially at night. Flora of China records the species across its native range.

## In Chinese tradition

- **Materia medica:** 金银花 is a well-known flower in traditional Chinese medicine (recorded in the 本草 materials), where it is traditionally associated with clearing heat. This is **traditional materia-medica language, not modern clinical evidence.**
- **Not a 香乘 incense ingredient:** 金银花 does not appear in the classical incense compendium 香乘; its role in incense is a modern crossover from its aromatic and materia-medica profile.
- **Scented tea and flavouring:** the flower is used in some scented teas and herbal drinks for its sweet fragrance.

## Traditional use vs modern evidence

The materia-medica identity is documented; its medicinal uses are **traditional**, not established clinical fact, and we make **no medical claims**. The documented, non-medical facts are botanical identity, the Caprifoliaceae placement, and the sweet honeyed floral aroma.

## Quality and sourcing notes

Practical points for buyers:

- 金银花 is the flower of *Lonicera japonica*; expect a light, honeyed, green-sweet floral rather than an opulent blossom.
- In incense it is a modern floral note, usually blended rather than burned alone; it is not a classical 香乘 ingredient.
- Dried flowers should be clean and retain a sweet, slightly green fragrance; browned or musty blooms are lower grade.

## Safety

Incense combustion produces smoke and particulate matter. Burn in a well-ventilated space, avoid inhaling concentrated smoke, and keep away from children and pets. This is editorial knowledge content, not medical advice.
