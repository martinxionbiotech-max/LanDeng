**Technical answer:** 米仔兰 — *mǐzǎilán* in Chinese — is a small evergreen tree or shrub of the mahogany family (Meliaceae), *Aglaia odorata*, native to southern China and Southeast Asia. Its tiny, yellow, bead-like flowers are intensely and sweetly fragrant, and it is grown across the subtropical south as a garden and courtyard fragrant plant. It is a **southern Chinese fragrant flower** — but it does **not** appear in the Ming-dynasty *Xiangcheng* (香乘), and this page records that absence honestly.

## Aroma profile

米仔兰 is sweet and floral with a delicate, slightly fruity (often described as lemon- or tea-adjacent) character — a fine-grained, intimate fragrance carried on tiny clusters of bead-sized yellow flowers. It is softer and less heady than jasmine or champaca, closer to a subtle, sweet garden scent. In practice it is a *living-plant fragrance*, enjoyed in the garden or courtyard rather than as a burned material.

## A southern flower, honestly placed (read this)

米仔兰 belongs to the garden, not to the classical incense compendium. Despite its southern popularity and the word 兰 ("orchid") in several of its common names — 米仔兰, 米兰, 树兰, 珠兰 — it is **not an orchid** (Orchidaceae) but a mahogany-family tree (*Aglaia odorata*, Meliaceae), and it is **absent from the Ming-dynasty *Xiangcheng* (香乘)**. The 兰 in its name is a fragrance honorific shared by many unrelated sweet-scented plants, not a botanical or a classical-incense claim.

## Botanical source and origin

*Aglaia odorata* is a small evergreen tree or shrub of the mahogany family (Meliaceae), native to southern China and Southeast Asia and long cultivated as an ornamental and fragrant plant across the subtropical south. Its flowers are tiny, round, and yellow, borne in loose clusters, and are the fragrant part; the name 米仔兰 ("rice-grain orchid") and 米兰 ("rice orchid") describe the bead-sized flowers. Flora of China records the species.

## In Chinese culture

- **Courtyard and garden fragrance:** 米仔兰 is planted for its sweet, delicate flower scent, enjoyed in gardens, courtyards, and as a potted fragrant plant in the south.
- **The 兰 honorific:** its common names (米仔兰, 米兰, 树兰) carry 兰, a fragrance honorific shared by many unrelated sweet-scented plants — not a botanical orchid.
- **Not a 香乘 ingredient:** 米仔兰 has no entry in the Ming-dynasty *Xiangcheng* (香乘), and its use is as a living fragrant plant, not a burned incense material.
