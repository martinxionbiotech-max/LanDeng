## Botanical source and origin

*Viola odorata*, the sweet violet, is a low perennial herb of the violet family (Violaceae), native to Europe and western Asia. Its small, purple flowers are the fragrant part; the scent is delicate and famously fleeting, so modern perfumery often recreates it with orris (iris) root. GBIF records the species. It is distinct from the garden stock (*Matthiola incana*, Brassicaceae) that also goes by 紫罗兰 in Chinese.

## In modern use

- **Perfumery:** the violet note is a classic soft, powdery floral in fine fragrance, often rendered with orris root or synthetic ionones.
- **Home fragrance:** violet scents appear in modern home fragrance and cosmetics.
- **Not classical Chinese incense:** violet has no place in the classical 香材 canon; its Chinese name and use are modern.
