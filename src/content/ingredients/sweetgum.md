# Sweetgum (枫香 / Feng Xiang)

**Direct answer:** Sweetgum — *fēngxiāng* (枫香) in Chinese — is the aromatic resin of the sweetgum tree, *Liquidambar formosana* (family Altingiaceae). It has a sweet, balsamic, warm aroma and is used in incense and perfumery, where it is closely related to (but distinct from) storax.

## Aroma profile

Sweetgum resin has a sweet, warm, balsamic scent with a resinous depth — soft and comforting, in the same broad family as benzoin but with its own character. It reads as a sweet base or supporting note rather than a bright top.

## The storax distinction (buyer intelligence)

"Sweetgum" resin and "storax" are often confused, because both come from *Liquidambar* trees:

- **枫香脂 (fēngxiāngzhī)** — resin of *Liquidambar formosana*, the Chinese sweetgum. This is the material most often meant by "sweetgum" in a Chinese incense context.
- **苏合香 / storax** — resin of *Liquidambar orientalis* (Oriental sweetgum), the classic "storax" of perfumery.

They are related resins from related trees, but not the same product. When a label says "sweetgum" or "storax," the species matters for the exact aroma and price.

## In Chinese tradition

- **Incense (香):** 枫香脂 is a sweet-balsamic ingredient in Chinese 合香, adding warmth and roundness.
- **Traditional use:** the resin has been used as an aromatic and in traditional preparations.
- **Cultural note:** 枫 (maple/sweetgum) trees carry their own seasonal and literary associations in Chinese culture.

## Traditional use vs modern evidence

Sweetgum resin appears in traditional uses; we describe its aromatic role and make **no medical claims**. Its perfumery value is a matter of aroma and fixative character, not health benefit.

## Safety

Incense combustion produces smoke and particulate matter; burn in a well-ventilated space and avoid inhaling concentrated smoke. This is editorial knowledge content, not medical advice.

## FAQ

**Is sweetgum the same as storax?** Closely related but not identical — sweetgum resin (枫香脂) comes from *Liquidambar formosana*, while storax (苏合香) comes from *Liquidambar orientalis*. They are related resins from related trees.

**Is sweetgum a wood or a resin?** In incense, "sweetgum" means the resin (枫香脂) tapped from the tree, not the wood itself.

## People Also Ask (Long-Tail)

**What does sweetgum smell like?** Sweet, warm, and balsamic with a resinous depth — a soft, comforting base note in the benzoin family.

**What is sweetgum resin used for in incense?** It adds a sweet-balsamic warmth to blends and acts as a supporting/fixative note, rounding out woods and resins.

**How do you burn sweetgum resin?** Like other resins — on a lit charcoal disc (in sand or ash) or gently on an electric incense heater, using small pieces at a time.
