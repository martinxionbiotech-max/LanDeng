## R-001 findings summary

- **沉香** = "sinking fragrance"; **奇楠** = top grade (油包木 "oil wraps wood" vs 沉香 木包油 "wood wraps oil").
- Grading: 沉水 (sinks) > 半沉半浮 (half) > floating.
- 奇楠 five color grades: 白/绿/紫/黄/黑.
- Adulteration: oil-boiling, high-pressure oil injection, dye-soaking, dyed white wood.
- Medical governance: 本草纲目 claims are TRADITIONAL_BELIEF, not clinical evidence.
- Conflicts: 奇楠 etymology + color-grade hierarchy (aging stages vs distinct grades).
