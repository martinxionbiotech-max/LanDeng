# CONTENT-GOVERNANCE.md — LanDeng

Editorial rules, evidence discipline, and the publish gate. This file is authoritative for every page.

## 1. Medical / health claim governance

**Never** claim: treats/cures insomnia · treats anxiety · improves depression · "medical sleep aid" · "clinically proven" · "guaranteed relaxation" · "natural = safe".

**Use instead:** evening ritual · aromatic experience · calming atmosphere · traditional use · meditation setting · mindfulness practice · home fragrance · sensory experience.

**Combustion risk must be disclosed.** Incense burns and produces smoke/particulate matter; safety content is mandatory, transparent, and never minimized.

## 2. Evidence discipline

Every important claim is classified:

`FACT · TRADITIONAL_BELIEF · HISTORICAL_RECORD · CULTURAL_INTERPRETATION · EXPERT_OPINION · INDUSTRY_PRACTICE · SCIENTIFIC_FINDING · PRELIMINARY_EVIDENCE · UNVERIFIED_CLAIM · CONFLICTING_SOURCES`

Evidence levels: `Traditional · Historical · Experimental · Preliminary · Human Research · Established Evidence · No Established Evidence`.

**Never upgrade** a traditional belief → scientific fact. **Never hide** uncertainty or conflict.

## 3. Editorial principles (unified)

1. **Research-grade ≠ clinical-grade.** A source review is not a clinical trial.
2. **Chemical identity ≠ commercial name.** A botanical's scientific identity is not the same as a trade name.
3. **Mechanistic evidence ≠ therapeutic evidence.** "Contains compound X" is not "treats condition Y".
4. **Delete unverifiable precise numbers.** If a figure can't be sourced, drop it or mark it UNVERIFIED.
5. **No absence claims** ("there is no evidence" without a defined search scope).
6. **No Wikipedia as a reference** for substantive claims (use as a discovery pointer only).
7. **Show conflicts** — never silently pick the convenient source.
8. **No fabricated** reviews, ratings, prices, stock, authors, certifications, test results, or research.

## 4. Information-gain rule

**No information gain = no page.** Before creating any page, answer:
1. What already exists online?
2. What does LanDeng add?
3. What original info/analysis/first-party knowledge exists?
4. What Chinese knowledge is missing from English?
5. What user problem is solved?
6. Why should Google rank this? Why would an AI cite it? Why would anyone link it?

If the only answer is "we translated existing content" → **do not publish.**

## 5. Originality rule

Acceptable "what did we add": original analysis · structured synthesis · first-party data · original framework · original comparison · Chinese knowledge analyzed (not translated) · original photography · manufacturing knowledge · better taxonomy · better terminology.

## 6. Source quality (S1–S5)

- **S1** Primary/authoritative (academic, government, museum, standards, primary records)
- **S2** Institutional/professional (universities, institutes)
- **S3** Expert/specialist (practitioners, specialist publications, manufacturers)
- **S4** Industry/commercial (manufacturers, suppliers, trade press — bias considered)
- **S5** General web/consumer (terminology, user questions, trends — not evidence)

Important topics: **3–10 independent sources**, with consensus/conflict/uncertainty documented.

## 7. Chinese-language sources

Chinese sources are a strategic advantage, not a copy source. Research → analyze → synthesize → original English content. Never direct-translate Chinese pages. See CHINESE-SOURCE-INTELLIGENCE.md.

## 8. AI governance

AI may assist: research, source discovery, classification, entity/terminology extraction, outlines, metadata, internal linking, structured data, first drafts.

AI must not replace: source verification, fact-checking, editorial judgment, original analysis, first-party validation.

**Quality > Quantity. Originality > Scale. Entity depth > Keyword count. Information gain > Word count.**
