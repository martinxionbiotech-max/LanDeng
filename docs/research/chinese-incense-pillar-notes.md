## History timeline (consensus across sources)

| Period | Marker |
|---|---|
| Shang (1600–1046 BCE) | aromatic plants burned as offerings; 香 on oracle bones |
| Han (206 BCE–220 CE) | **博山炉** Boshan censer — incense moves from ritual → aesthetic |
| Wei-Jin / N+S Dynasties (220–589) | Buddhism + Silk Road/maritime trade import 沉香/檀香/龙脑/乳香/没药/丁香; 合香 system forms |
| Sui-Tang (581–907) | first golden age; court luxury; 合香 (blended incense) matures |
| **Song (960–1279)** | **THE PEAK** — 四般闲事; 隔火焚香; 合香 refined; 香谱 manuals written |
| Ming (1368–1644) | codification; 宣德炉 Xuande furnace; 线香 (stick incense) perfected |
| Qing (1644–1912) → 20th c. | decline: sumptuary laws, Western perfume, Cultural Revolution ("四旧") near-extinction; revival from 2000s |

## Core terminology (high-value, poorly systemized in English)

- **四般闲事** (sì bān xián shì) — "Four Leisure Arts": 烧香 (incense) · 点茶 (tea) · 挂画 (hanging scrolls) · 插花 (flower arranging). Source: 吴自牧《梦粱录》: "烧香点茶，挂画插花，四般闲事，不宜累家."
- **香道** (xiāngdào) — "the Way of Incense." **Misconception to correct:** widely assumed to be Japanese; it originated in China, and Japan's kodo (香道) evolved from the Song technique 隔火焚香.
- **隔火焚香** (gé huǒ fén xiāng) — "indirect-fire incense": bury hot charcoal in ash, set a silver/mica plate above, rest the material on the plate — no flame, minimal smoke, pure aroma. The predecessor of Japanese kodo.
- **篆香 / 打香印** (zhuàn xiāng) — "seal incense": powder pressed into a pattern/stamp, burns along a timed path (visual + timekeeping).
- **合香** (hé xiāng) — "blended/compound incense" (multi-ingredient), built on a 君臣佐使 (chief/minister/assistant/guide) formula principle analogous to TCM compounding. (Already in glossary.)
- **香谱** (xiāng pǔ) — incense manuals: 陈氏香谱, 洪芻香谱, 新纂香谱, 周嘉胄《香乘》. World's most complete ancient incense literature, mostly untranslated.

## The "middle path" positioning (from English SERP)

Chinese incense sits between **Indian agarbatti** (bold, expressive) and **Japanese koh** (minimalist refinement): elegant but warm, complex but not overwhelming. (Cenisy / Serene Orient framing.)
