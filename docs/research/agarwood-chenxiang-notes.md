## Core facts

### Botanical
- Agarwood = resin-impregnated heartwood of **Aquilaria** trees (Thymelaeaceae), formed in response to wounding/fungal infection. Healthy Aquilaria wood is pale, light, scentless.
- Species: **Aquilaria sinensis** (Chinese agarwood / 白木香), **Aquilaria malaccensis** (classic import, now depleted), **Aquilaria agallocha** (synonym used in trade).
- Resin forms over years–decades; naturally induced in trees >20 years old; now also cultivated/induced by wounding.

### Etymology (key terminology)
- **沉香** (chénxiāng) = "sinking fragrance" — the best pieces are resin-dense enough to **sink in water**.
- **奇楠 / 伽楠 / 伽罗 / 棋楠 / 琪楠 / 奇蓝** (qínán) = top grade of agarwood; name Sanskrit-derived (disputed: "black" vs "temple" etymology). **English has no good equivalent** — "kyara" (Japanese) is the closest cognate.

### Grading — 沉水 system (Chinese, poorly documented in English)
- **沉水 (sinks)** = highest resin content, most valuable
- **半沉半浮 (half-sinks)** = medium
- **floating** = lower (historically "huang shu xiang" 黄熟香)

### 奇楠 vs 普通沉香 (the key distinction English misses)
- 沉香 = **"wood wraps oil" (木包油)** — hard, resin veins in hard wood
- 奇楠 = **"oil wraps wood" (油包木)** — wood fiber fully transformed by resin; soft/pliable; **"shaves into curls, kneads into pellets" (削之成卷、捏之成丸)**; aromatic even at room temperature
- 奇楠 grading by color (five grades): **白奇楠 (white) · 绿奇楠/莺歌绿 (green) · 紫奇楠/兰花结 (purple) · 黄奇楠/金丝结 (yellow) · 黑奇楠/铁结 (black)**

### Formation types
熟结 (dead tree, buried/aged) · 生结 (living tree) · 虫漏 (insect bore) · 脱落 (external wound/break)

### Adulteration (high-value buyer-intelligence content)
Common fakes: oil-boiling (煮油), high-pressure oil injection (高压灌油), chemical/dye soaking (泡药水), dyed non-resinous "white wood," foreign woods (Lauraceae/Burseraceae) with synthetic fragrance.
**Authenticity checks:** white smoke + oil seepage when burned (vs black smoke for fakes); distinct complex sweet aroma when gently heated; 沉水 test; no "burnt wood / perfume" smell.

### Medical governance (critical — TCM claims)
Chinese sources (本草纲目) attribute "行气止痛，温中降逆，补五脏" (move qi, relieve pain, warm the middle, tonify). **This is traditional materia-medica language, NOT modern clinical evidence.** Any published content must classify these as TRADITIONAL_BELIEF / HISTORICAL_RECORD, never as therapeutic fact. We do **not** repeat "boosts immunity / improves heart" as if proven.

## Conflicts / uncertainty
- 奇楠 name etymology: "black" (梵语) vs "temple/伽蓝" — disputed across sources.
- 奇楠 color-grade hierarchy: whether 紫棋/黄棋 are distinct grades or aging stages of 绿棋 — sources disagree.
- "奇楠 sinks or floats": some sources say top 奇楠 is 半沉半浮 (not 沉水) because its resin is soft/evenly distributed — contradicts the "sinking = best" rule for ordinary agarwood. **This is a genuine distinction English content misses.**
