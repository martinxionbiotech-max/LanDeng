# ORGANIC-ACQUISITION.md — LanDeng

Internal management framework for organic acquisition. **Not a fake "SEO score".** A scorecard, updated month-over-month and quarter-over-quarter.

## Scorecard dimensions

| Dimension | What it measures |
|---|---|
| SEO visibility | impressions, clicks, CTR, position, non-brand queries |
| AI visibility | AI Overview / AI-mode presence, AI-referenced pages/entities, AI referral traffic |
| Entity authority | entity coverage and recognition |
| Topical authority | cluster coverage, depth, question coverage |
| Organic landing surface | indexable, intent-mapped pages |
| Commercial intent coverage | wholesale/OEM/sample/quote landing coverage |
| Natural authority | natural backlinks, brand mentions, citations, referring domains |
| Organic conversion | quote/sample/wholesale/OEM/private-label inquiries |

## Cadence

- **Weekly:** Search Console changes, new queries/questions, indexing issues, conversion issues
- **Monthly:** topical authority, entity coverage, organic growth, AI visibility, authority, leads, experiments
- **Quarterly:** architecture review, content pruning, topic consolidation, entity expansion, roadmap

## KPI (primary)

**Qualified organic leads.** Secondary: organic revenue, organic quote/sample requests, organic wholesale/OEM leads.

## Rules

- No unsupported ranking guarantees.
- Optimize for **qualified demand**, not traffic / keyword count / article count.
