# ART-DIRECTION.md — LanDeng

Visual identity for a brand that is "quiet, premium, natural, intelligent."

## Direction

Traditional Chinese material culture + modern botanical aesthetics + premium fragrance + editorial publishing + scientific credibility + modern luxury.

## Desired qualities

quiet · premium · natural · intelligent · editorial · tactile · authentic · sophisticated · spacious · restrained.

## Avoid

- generic WordPress / Shopify look
- cheap stock imagery
- excessive red + gold (cliché "Chinese")
- cliché Chinese motifs (dragons, lanterns everywhere, chop-stamp overload)
- generic spa aesthetics
- fake luxury
- excessive gradients, glassmorphism, animation, clutter

## Brand motif

**澜灯 — "lantern on the waves."** Light + water as the quiet conceptual motif (not literal decoration): a soft glow over still water. Translate into restrained visual language — warm amber accents on deep neutral surfaces, generous whitespace, subtle texture.

## Photography direction (original preferred)

Raw botanicals · herbs · wood · resin · flowers · powder · materials · processing (drying, rolling, cutting) · making · packaging · burning · smoke · holders · products · factory · hands · macro material shots · traditional context · editorial still life.

**Original visual evidence > generic stock.**

## Layout

Editorial, magazine-like. Generous whitespace, clear hierarchy, restrained color, typography-led. Science/research sections get a cleaner, more neutral treatment (credibility), while culture/ritual sections get warmth (texture, amber).
