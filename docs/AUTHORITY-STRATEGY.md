# AUTHORITY-STRATEGY.md — LanDeng

Authority is earned by publishing **link-worthy information**, not by chasing backlinks.

## Link-worthy asset classes

- Original classifications / taxonomies (e.g. Chinese incense material classification)
- Original databases (ingredient / material / aroma / terminology)
- Original comparisons (materials, formats, Chinese vs Western)
- Original research summaries (with honest evidence labeling)
- Original terminology systems (Chinese–English)
- Original photography (raw botanicals, processing, burning)
- First-party manufacturing knowledge
- Original data (first-party observations)

## Discovery surfaces

One strong asset should generate multiple discovery paths:

Google Search · AI Search · Images · Research citations · Industry references · Community discussions · Natural links · Brand searches · Direct · Product discovery.

## Principles

- **Never** buy links intended to manipulate rankings.
- **Never** use link schemes, microsite networks, or mass guest posting.
- **Never** fabricate authority (fake reviews, fake certifications, fake authors).
- Build **natural citations + natural links + brand mentions + entity recognition**.

## Milestones

1. Publish a genuinely citable original asset (taxonomy / database / terminology system).
2. Ensure entity clarity so the brand is recognized as a coherent entity.
3. Track referring domains, brand mentions, citations (see ORGANIC-ACQUISITION.md).
