# 澜灯 LanDeng — Chinese Botanical Incense

**An English-language knowledge platform + commercial lead engine for Chinese botanical incense.**

LanDeng (澜灯, "lantern on the waves") is a research-driven digital knowledge company. We document Chinese incense — its materials, botanicals, history, terminology, craft and culture — and translate that knowledge into original, machine-readable, search- and AI-discoverable English content, connected to a DTC / wholesale / OEM / private-label commercial layer.

## Positioning

- **Core entity:** Chinese botanical incense (香 / 熏香 / 合香)
- **Not:** a sleep-medicine site, an insomnia-treatment site, an anxiety-treatment site, a generic aromatherapy store.
- **"Evening ritual" is a use-case cluster, not a medical claim.**

## Markets

United States · United Kingdom · Europe · Canada · Australia · other English-speaking markets.

## Acquisition model

Google organic search + AI search/AI discovery + organic referrals + natural citations — **not** paid ads, social spam, guest-post networks, or artificial links.

## Commercial model (Phase 1: no online payment)

DTC · Small wholesale · Wholesale · OEM · Private Label.

Primary conversions: **Request a Sample · Request a Quote · Wholesale Inquiry · OEM Inquiry · Private Label Inquiry · Contact.**

## Technical stack

- **Astro** (static generation) — knowledge / SEO / AIO / product content
- **WooCommerce** (REST API) — product/SKU/attribute source of truth, future pricing/orders
- **Phase 1:** catalog + knowledge + inquiry only (no cart/checkout/accounts/payment)

## Documentation-first workflow

This repository follows a strict documentation-first gate. **No website is built until the architecture documents are approved.** The `docs/` directory is the single source of truth for strategy, models, inventories and governance.
