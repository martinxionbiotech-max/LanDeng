# Which Incense Format Is Right for You? Sticks, Cones, Coils, Powder & Beads

**Direct answer:** The main incense formats are **sticks** (the everyday default), **backflow cones** (a visual waterfall effect), **coils** (hours-long burn), **powder & resin** (loose material on heat), and **aromatherapy beads** (scented bracelets, no burning). The right one depends on **burn time, smoke, and whether you want a visual**.

**Key facts:** A stick burns roughly 20–60 minutes; a cone ~10–15 minutes; a coil can burn for hours; resin is heated, not burned (least smoke); beads are worn, not burned (no smoke at all).

---

## Key Takeaways

- **Sticks** are the default — predictable, ~20–60 min, low-effort.
- **Cones** are short (~10–15 min) and often chosen for the **backflow** visual effect.
- **Coils** burn for **hours** — ideal for long, steady scenting.
- **Powder & resin** are heated (not burned) for a **smoke-light, pure-material** scent.
- **Aromatherapy beads** are **worn**, not burned — a portable, zero-smoke option.

| Beads | n/a (worn) | None | Portable scent, no burning |

---

## How to Choose

1. **Start with burn time.** A short session (meditation) → stick or cone; an all-day scent → coil; a "no smoke" preference → resin on a heater or beads.
2. **Do you want a visual?** Backflow cones are chosen almost entirely for their smoke waterfall.
3. **How pure do you want the material?** Resin/powder on heat gives the closest-to-material scent; a stick is more convenient but may carry a binder and (sometimes) fragrance oil.

## FAQ

### Q: Are incense sticks or cones better?
Neither is "better" — a stick burns longer (~20–60 min), a cone is shorter (~10–15 min). Pick by session length. Backflow cones are a special case chosen for their visual.

### Q: How long does an incense coil burn?
A coil can burn for hours (often several), making it the format for long, steady room scent. See [incense coils](/incense-coils/).

### Q: What burns the longest?
Coils burn the longest by far; sticks are ~20–60 min and cones ~10–15 min. See [incense coils](/incense-coils/).

### Q: What is a backflow cone?
A hollow cone that directs smoke downward to create a waterfall effect — chosen for its visual, not its scent. See [backflow cones](/backflow-cones/).
