# The 香谱 Incense Manuals: The World's Most Complete (and Least Translated) Incense Literature

**Direct answer:** The 香谱 (xiāng pǔ) — "incense manuals" — are the canonical Chinese texts that record incense formulas, materials, and lore, culminating in the Ming-dynasty encyclopedia 《香乘》 by Zhou Jiazhou. They are widely regarded as the **world's most complete ancient incense literature**, and they remain **almost entirely untranslated** into English.

**Key facts:** The major manuals include 陈氏香谱 (Chen's Incense Manual), 洪芻香谱 (Hong Chu's), 新纂香谱 (the New Compilation), and 《香乘》 (the Incense Compendium). Their untranslated status is the single biggest gap in English-language incense knowledge.

## The Major Manuals

| Manual | Chinese | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 陈氏香谱 | Chén shì xiāng pǔ | "Chen's Incense Manual" — a foundational text |
| 洪芻香谱 | Hóng Chú xiāng pǔ | Hong Chu's manual |
| 新纂香谱 | Xīn zuǎn xiāng pǔ | The "New Compilation" |
| 香乘 | Xiāng chéng | Zhou Jiazhou's Ming-dynasty encyclopedia — the most comprehensive |

The 香乘 (literally "Incense Vehicle/Compendium") is often treated as the capstone: an encyclopedic collection that gathers the earlier manuals into one reference.

---

## Why These Texts Matter

1. **They are the primary record** of the 合香 blending tradition — hundreds of named formulas, not just ingredient lists. (See [the blending system](/hexiang-blending-system/).)
2. **They document the material culture** — what was burned, how, and for what purpose, across centuries.
3. **They are the source** for the terminology and grading systems (香道, 合香, 奇楠) that English content struggles with.

---

## The Translation Gap (and Why It Matters)

The 香谱 are almost entirely **untranslated into English**. This is not a small detail — it is the reason the English internet's understanding of Chinese incense is so thin:

- The **formulas** are inaccessible to non-Chinese readers.
- The **terminology** (合香, 奇楠, 沉水) has no canonical English rendering.
- The **depth** — that Chinese incense is a formula art with a written canon — is largely invisible in English.

A knowledge hub that systematically translates and organizes this material is doing something no competitor is doing. That is the strategic value of this content.
