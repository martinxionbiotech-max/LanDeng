# Aromatherapy Beads & Scented Bracelets: Worn Scent, No Smoke

**Direct answer:** Aromatherapy beads are porous beads (often wood, stone, or clay) that are **scented with essential oil and worn**, so the aroma is released slowly as a personal scent — with **no burning and no smoke**. They are the portable, zero-smoke option in the incense family.

**Key facts:** The beads themselves are usually not the scent source; they are a **carrier** you (or the maker) scent with essential oil. The scent fades over time and is refreshed by re-applying oil. This is a *worn-fragrance* format, not a burned one.

---

## Key Takeaways

- Aromatherapy beads are **worn**, not burned — zero smoke, zero flame.
- They are a **carrier** scented with essential oil; the scent fades and is refreshed.
- It is a **personal** scent (close to you), not a room-filling one.
- No therapeutic effect is claimed — this is a fragrance and comfort object.

---

## What They Are

Aromatherapy beads (also called scent beads, oil beads, or fragrance bracelets) are small beads strung into bracelets or necklaces. The bead material is porous — sandalwood, other woods, lava stone, or clay — so it absorbs and slowly releases essential oil.

Two things to be clear about:

1. **The scent comes from the oil, not the bead.** A "sandalwood bead bracelet" is usually scented wood, not the raw sandalwood heartwood.
2. **The scent fades.** After days or weeks, you re-apply a drop of oil to refresh it. The "recharge" is part of the format.

---

## Beads vs. Burned Incense

| | Beads | Stick / cone |
|---|---|---|
| Scent delivery | Worn, close to you | Room-filling |
| Smoke / flame | None | Smoke, open flame |
| Duration | Days–weeks (fading, refreshable) | Minutes–hours |
| Best for | Personal scent, no-smoke settings | Scenting a space |

Beads solve the problem of wanting a scent in a **no-smoke environment** (offices, shared spaces, travel) or wanting a scent **on you** rather than in the room.

## Common Mistakes

1. **Expecting the bead to be the scent.** It is a carrier; the oil is the scent.
2. **Expecting the scent to last.** It fades and needs refreshing.
3. **Applying undiluted essential oil directly to skin.** Oils are potent; a bead is a safer carrier than skin, but still use care.

## FAQ

### Q: Do aromatherapy beads produce smoke?
No — they are worn and release scent slowly from essential oil; there is no burning and no smoke.

### Q: How long does the scent last?
Days to weeks, fading over time, and refreshed by re-applying a drop of essential oil. The bead is reusable.

### Q: What are aromatherapy beads made of?
Porous materials — often wood (including sandalwood), lava stone, or clay — that absorb and slowly release the oil.

### Q: Are aromatherapy beads the same as incense?
They are in the same fragrance family but a different format: worn and zero-smoke, versus burned and room-filling. See [which incense format](/which-incense-format/).
